# Intake questions — "See What We Can Improve"

Use these on the first call or reply. Keep it a conversation, not a questionnaire. The public page
shows a short version (7 questions); this is the full working list.

1. **Where do your leads come from today?** (website form, email, phone, Google profile, social, referrals)
2. **Who responds to them?** One person, a few, whoever is free?
3. **How long does a first response usually take?** And on a busy day?
4. **Which tools does your team use day to day?** (email provider, calendar, spreadsheet, CRM/job software, texting)
5. **What gets copied by hand from one place to another?**
6. **Where do leads or follow-ups get lost?** Can you remember a recent one?
7. **Which steps do you repeat for every new lead?**
8. **What do you wish happened automatically?**
9. **What must always require a person's approval?** (pricing, scheduling, anything sent to a customer)

Notes for NLDS:
- Q9 sets the human approval points in the onboarding checklist. Never skip it.
- If the answers point to a website problem, say so. Not every bottleneck needs a workflow.
- If they only need incoming lead response, mention LeadStrike as the separate option.
- The contact form is not changed for this. The form has a "Business Workflow or Process Problem"
  option, and the page asks visitors to include answers in their message.
